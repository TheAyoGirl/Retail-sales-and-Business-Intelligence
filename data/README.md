Dataset: Sample Superstore
The dataset represents a fictional retail business and contains transactional information covering:
•	Orders 
•	Customers 
•	Products 
•	Sales 
•	Profit 
•	Quantity 
•	Discounts 
•	Shipping 
•	Geography 
•	Product categories 
•	Returns 
•	Regional managers 
The analysis covers 2023–2026.
